3. Photo Processing

All photo processing uses the HTML5 Canvas API running entirely in your browser. When you use the AI background removal feature with Remove.bg, your image is sent directly from your browser to Remove.bg's servers — not through ours. Review Remove.bg's privacy policy separately.

🔒 You can verify local processing: open your browser's Network tab — no image data is transmitted to passportsnap.com servers.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data. To opt out of analytics, use browser settings or a privacy extension. Contact us at privacy@passportsnap.com to exercise any right.
